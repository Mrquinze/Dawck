# Changelog

## Dawck 3.0 Cypriot Vanilla


### Look & feel
- **New renderer** – docks are drawn by a new engine. Magnifying, appearing, hiding and glowing now hold a steady 60 fps, and hovering costs a fraction of what it did.
- **No more box around your docks** – the faint rectangular layer behind each dock is gone; blur and shadow now follow the dock's own shape exactly.
- **Liquid Glass** – choose Apple's own Liquid Glass (clear or frosted) for any dock, or let Dawck's custom glass follow the new macOS 27 clear/opaque slider. The settings window is glass too.
- **Designs & palettes** – twenty palettes (including Goorgle's) and a gallery of designs, from crystal pills to flat paper. Edit up to five colours and the angle yourself. Save the current look to reuse it, or give one dock a different look than the rest.

### Thirteen new widgets
Analogue clock, world clock, month calendar, GPU load, network speed, IP address (local and public), device batteries, thermal state, volume, keyboard layout, keep-awake, countdown timer and weather. Widgets only measure while they are on a dock that is showing, and widgets that use the internet are marked with a globe.

### Docks that behave
- **Docks that step aside** – hide a dock while an app is full screen or a window covers it, and bring it back by pushing the pointer to the screen edge. Each dock gets its own show/hide shortcut.
- **Edit mode & locking** – lock a dock so a stray drag can't change it, then right-click → Edit Dock to rearrange, add and remove with the familiar minus badges.
- **Drag & drop** – drop files on an app to open them with it, on a folder to move them in (hold ⌥ to copy), on a tray to stash them, or on the Trash to throw them away.
- **Better folders** – open as a grid, list or fan, sorted your way, with hidden files on request and real previews of pictures, PDFs and documents.
- **Live icons & friendlier clicks** – Calendar and Clock tiles show today's date and the time; clicking an app with no windows opens a new one, and launching apps bounce.
- **Multiple displays** – show one dock on every screen, or mirror your system Dock on a second display and hide the apps you don't want there.
- **Make my extra docks the same size** – copies icon size and magnification to every Dawck dock so they sit comfortably beside the system Dock.

### Safer & lighter
- **Lighter on your Mac** – everything is event-driven: nothing polls while you aren't looking at it, and the settings window is built only when you open it.
- **Safer profile import** – profiles from other people arrive with their actions switched off until you approve them, and every imported value is checked and size-limited.
- **Backups** – docks, themes and profiles are backed up, with a daily copy kept whenever something has changed.
- **Getting Started** – a new welcome tour with "What's New & Getting Started", shown on first launch and (optionally) after updates.

Requires macOS 14.0 or later. As with previous versions, this build is signed but not notarized: right-click `Dawck.app` → **Open** the first time.
